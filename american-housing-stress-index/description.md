#### American Housing Stress Index

This project measures housing stress across census tracts using a weighted index.

The index is a product of the number of homes in each census tract and the percentage of each home's monthly income spent on housing.

The index returns a score between -4 and 3, the lower the value, the less stressed the tract.

A lower number means most homes in the tract spend less the fewest percentage of their income on housing; a higher number means

more of a home's total income is being spent on housing.
